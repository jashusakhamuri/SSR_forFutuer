Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Draft for Public Operations

Founder Notice: This privacy document outlines our static website privacy practices and WhatsApp order handling in accordance with Indian Information Technology regulations. Legal counsel review recommended before commercial scaling.

1. Overview and Scope

SSR Foods ("SSR", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how that information is handled during your WhatsApp ordering interaction, and how we safeguard your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We deliberately minimize the personal data we collect. Our static website does not require account creation or password storage.

  • Delivery & Contact Details: When you prepare an order via our checkout form, we collect your Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, Delivery Address (State, District, Village/Town, House No./Street, Landmark, and 6-digit PIN code), and optional delivery notes.
  • Client-Side Local Storage: To prevent you from having to re-type your address upon revisits, our website may temporarily store your recent address in your own browser’s localStorage. You can clear this anytime through your browser settings or our "Clear Cart" controls.
  • No Payment Card Storage: SSR does not collect, store, or process debit/credit card numbers or netbanking passwords on this website.

3. WhatsApp Ordering Channel Handoff

When you click "Place Order via WhatsApp", the details you entered are encoded into a standard URL string that launches the official WhatsApp application.

The transmission of your message through WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp LLC's end-to-end encrypted privacy infrastructure. SSR uses your submitted phone number and delivery address strictly to confirm batch availability, calculate shipping charges, coordinate delivery, and provide customer support.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details to third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing syndicates.

Your delivery address is shared only with postal or courier partners involved in fulfilling and delivering your parcel.
