Shipping & Returns

Honest, straightforward delivery and replacement guidelines.

1. International Delivery

Worldwide delivery can be arranged through our team. International orders are handled directly on WhatsApp; the online checkout currently accepts Indian addresses only. Message us with your destination and selected blends so we can confirm service availability, the shipping quote and estimated transit time before payment.

Destination customs or import charges may apply separately and are determined by the destination country.

2. Domestic Shipping & Delivery Timelines

SSR ships directly across India utilizing trusted surface and speed logistics partners, including India Post Speed Post and regional couriers.

  • Processing Time: Orders confirmed before 1:00 PM IST on working days are packed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Estimated Transit Time: Metro cities and South Indian regional hubs typically receive deliveries within 3 to 5 business days. Remote or rural PIN codes may require 5 to 8 business days depending on postal carrier connectivity.
  • Consignment Tracking: Once your consignment is dispatched, our team sends your tracking consignment number and carrier URL directly via WhatsApp.

3. Shipping Charges Policy

In keeping with our transparency commitment, SSR does not conceal shipping fees inside inflated spice prices or make unverified claims of "Free Nationwide Shipping":

Actual postal transit charges depend on total parcel weight (in grams/kg) and your destination PIN code zone. Our fulfillment team computes the exact carrier fee during our WhatsApp order review and quotes it for your final approval before you pay.

4. Order Cancellation

You may cancel your order at zero penalty at any point before the parcel is handed over to the courier partner. Send a message to our WhatsApp support line citing your order reference.

Once a consignment has been physically picked up by the logistics carrier and assigned a tracking bar code, it cannot be recalled or cancelled in transit.

5. Food Safety & Return / Replacement Policy

Under Indian Food Safety & Standards Authority (FSSAI) hygiene regulations, consumable food items and edible spices cannot be returned for restock once the outer security seal has been broken, as product integrity cannot be recertified.

However, customer satisfaction and trust are paramount:

  • Damaged or Leaking Parcels: If your consignment arrives with outer damage, tears, or broken pouch seals, take clear photographs of the package upon delivery and send them to us on WhatsApp within 48 hours.
  • Free Replacement or Full Refund: Upon quick verification of genuine transit damage or wrong pack delivery, SSR will immediately dispatch a fresh replacement batch at our cost or issue a prompt UPI refund.
